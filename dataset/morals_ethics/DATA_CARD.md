## 1. Motivation
- **Purpose.** ConsciousMind asks where AI systems match human judgment and where people still judge differently or better. For Morals and Ethics this needs
  (a) a large, documented pool of moral questions with the human judgments attached and (b) a way to separate clear-cut questions from contested ones.
  This dataset is that pool; `eval/` contains the first evaluation of local models against it.
- **Gap it fills.** Published moral benchmarks usually keep one label and drop the vote split, mix label types without saying so, and cover mostly everyday
  interpersonal ethics. Here every row keeps its original label, how it was produced, and the vote counts/agreement where the source published them.
- **Funding.** None; student research project.

## 2. Composition
- **Instances.** One row = one moral or ethical question: a scenario, a question, answer options, and (where available) a human-derived label.
- **Size.** 25,000 rows: 23,502 from published sources (seven datasets, plus 16 PhilPapers questions carried over from the earlier workbook) and 1,498 synthetic.

| Source | Rows | Text written by | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| ETHICS (commonsense, justice, deontology, virtue, utilitarianism) | 7,263 | Crowd workers (MTurk) | Crowd consensus (items kept only when annotators agreed) |
| Social Chemistry 101 | 6,397 | Reddit / advice-column / story situations; actions written by crowd workers | Crowd rating, 5 points (mostly one annotator) |
| Scruples Anecdotes | 4,390 | Reddit users (r/AmITheAsshole) | Plurality of Reddit commenters' votes (counts kept) |
| Scruples Dilemmas | 2,468 | Action pairs from Reddit titles | MTurk majority (counts kept) |
| DailyDilemmas | 1,354 | LLM-generated by the source authors | None |
| MoralChoice | 1,367 | 687 LLM-generated, 680 hand-written by authors | Author-intended answer (687); none (680) |
| MoralExceptQA | 132 | Study authors | Share of participants judging the action permissible (0.5 threshold) |
| BIG-bench simple_ethical_questions | 115 | Task authors | Author-designated |
| PhilPapers 2020 survey | 16 | Survey authors | Plurality of 1,785 philosophers (full distribution kept) |
| Synthetic applied ethics (this project) | 1,498 | Generated with Claude | None, by design |

- **Who wrote the text (`origin`).** human_authored 20,534; author_written 347; llm_generated_published 2,621; synthetic_this_project 1,498.
- **Labels (`human_label_status`).** human_labeled 20,666; author_designated 802; unlabeled 3,532.
  Label types: human_consensus 9,731; human_annotation (single annotator) 6,173; human_vote_plurality 4,614; dataset_design_intended 687;
  human_proportion_thresholded 132; author_designated 115; human_survey_plurality 16; none 3,532.
- **Splits.** train 17,514 / dev 2,467 / test 5,019, assigned per near-duplicate cluster so near-copies never cross splits (re-checked: 0 crossings).
- **Domains (`domain`).** interpersonal conflict 7,175; social norms / moral foundations 4,760; value-conflict dilemmas 4,634; justice 1,290; duties 1,289;
  well-being 1,288; everyday commonsense 1,143; virtue 1,105; harm avoidance / rule following 687; AI-alignment choices 115; philosophy positions 16;
  12 applied-ethics domains of about 125 each (synthetic only).
- **Missing information.** `n_annotators`, `label_agreement` and `label_distribution` are blank when the source did not publish them; labels are blank for open dilemmas.
- **Relationships.** Some Scruples Anecdotes, Scruples Dilemmas, Social Chemistry (AITA) and long ETHICS-commonsense items derive from the same Reddit posts.
  They are different tasks, but not independent samples.
- **Sensitive content.** Reddit posts describe real interpersonal conflicts (family, relationships, money) in users' own words. Some scenarios mention
  self-harm, violence, discrimination or animal cruelty, because the benchmarks test moral judgment about such acts. No personal identifiers were added;
  Reddit text is reproduced as the source distributed it.
- **Columns.** All 36 are listed in `README.md` and `PRESENTATION_GUIDE.md` section 6.

## 3. Collection process
- **How obtained.** Downloaded from the authors' public releases (GitHub repositories and Google Cloud Storage links of the Allen Institute for AI);
  ETHICS from a public GitHub mirror (all 400 ETHICS items of the earlier workbook match it in text and label). Commits and SHA-256 hashes are pinned in
  `work/fetch_manifest.json`. Hugging Face, Zenodo and arXiv could not be reached from the build environment.
- **Original collection** was done by each source's authors (see their papers in `papers/PAPERS.md`); this project collected no new human judgments.
- **Synthetic items.** 12 applied-ethics domains x 5 sub-topics x 25 scenarios were generated with Claude from `synthetic_prompt.md`
  (1,500 requested, 1,498 kept after automatic checks for length, option quality, answer-giving phrases, repeated openings and duplicate titles).
  Each has 2-3 options written to be defensible, no answer key, and was not reviewed by a person beyond a small manual read.
- **Timeframe.** Built 13 October 2026; source data dates from 2020 to 2024 publications.
- **Ethical review.** No new data from people was collected, so no review board was involved.

## 4. Preprocessing and cleaning
- Converted each source to one schema; original labels kept in `label_raw`, every change recorded in `label_transform` (e.g. ETHICS `1` -> `Yes`).
- Answer options shuffled with a fixed seed wherever a source puts the correct answer in a fixed position.
- Removed about 2,600 exact duplicates; dropped 601 records whose identical text had conflicting labels in the source; trimmed 15 near-duplicates
  (MinHash, word 3-grams, Jaccard >= 0.8, at most 2 kept per cluster); kept one Social Chemistry item per situation (544 dropped); 26 records failed validation.
- Large sources were sampled to fixed quotas with label-balanced stratification. **Consequence:** label frequencies in this file are not the natural
  frequencies of the sources (e.g. Social Chemistry ratings are near-uniform here, but mostly "expected/OK" in the source).
- Excluded after review: BIG-bench `moral_permissibility` (vignettes whose final question does not match the story; heavy templating), ETHICS `cm_ambig`
  (no labels), Scruples `train-extra` (different annotation regime).
- Raw downloads are not modified and not committed (re-download with `scripts/fetch_sources.py`). The full build is `scripts/run_all.sh`; a rebuild is byte-identical.

## 5. Uses
- **Intended.** Measuring how closely AI systems' moral judgments match human judgments, by source, label type and agreement; finding contested items
  where systems and people diverge, as candidates for a matched human-versus-AI study; studying refusals and answer-position effects.
- **Already used for.** `eval/`: Llama 3.1 8B, Qwen 2.5 7B and Gemma 2 9B on a 2,029-item stratified sample of the test split.
- **Not suitable for.** Treating labels as moral truth; estimating population opinion or base rates (sampling was label-balanced; labelers were mostly US,
  English-speaking, online); claiming a human advantage without a matched human baseline; training a "moral" classifier for deployment.
- **Contamination.** Almost all published items are public benchmarks likely seen in model training; only the synthetic items are new text.
